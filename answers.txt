Ques1) What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development?
Answer1) 

Pros of Agile:

Flexibility: Easily adapts to changing requirements.
Faster Delivery: Enables quick iterations and faster releases.

Cons of Agile:

Less Predictability: Hard to estimate timelines and costs accurately.
Requires High Collaboration: Demands constant communication, which can be challenging.

Ques2) Explain the role and responsibilities of Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.
Answer2)

The Scrum Master ensures the Scrum process runs smoothly by coaching the team on Agile practices, 
removing any obstacles that hinder progress, and fostering a productive, collaborative environment. 
They focus on improving team efficiency and maintaining workflow without directly managing the team.

The Product Owner defines the product vision, prioritizes the product backlog, and clearly communicates
requirements to the development team. They represent the customer’s interests, making sure the team 
focuses on building features that provide the most value to the business and end-users.

Ques3)What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle? List at least 2 points on 
what needs to be discussed during each of these meetings.
Answer3)

1. Sprint Planning Meeting:

Define Sprint Goals: Discuss what the team aims to achieve during the sprint.
Select Backlog Items: Identify and prioritize user stories or tasks from the product backlog to work on.

2. Daily Stand-up (Scrum) Meeting:

Progress Update: Each team member shares what they did yesterday and what they plan to do today.
Identify Blockers: Discuss any obstacles or issues preventing progress.

3. Sprint Retrospective Meeting:

What Went Well: Reflect on successes, effective processes, and positive team dynamics.
What Can Be Improved: Identify challenges faced during the sprint and suggest ways to improve in the next sprint.
